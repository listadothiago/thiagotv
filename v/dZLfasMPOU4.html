Fountains Of Wayne - Stacy's Mom (Official Music Video)

music // Aired 2026-07-01

Summary

A 2003 power-pop single, written with an obvious debt to The Cars, whose video parodies eighties teen-comedy conventions. The band had been working in this vein for years; this was the record that broke.

The craft is in the construction — a chorus that arrives quickly, a melody that resolves the way the ear expects — which is why it lodged so firmly despite being, in the band's own catalogue, far from their most ambitious song.

Why it matters

It is an object lesson in how a career of well-regarded, low-selling records can be recast by one hit, and in the mixed blessing that follows: the band remained best known for a novelty-adjacent single rather than the songwriting that preceded it.
