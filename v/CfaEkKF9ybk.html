films // Aired 2026-08-06

Summary

A 1977 American science fiction film featuring Christopher Lee, made on a small budget and now circulating freely online. It belongs to the wave of low-cost genre pictures that filled drive-ins and late-night television slots in the seventies.

Why it matters

Its interest today is largely as an artefact of how that market worked: a recognisable star attached to a modest production, distributed cheaply, and later surviving through television and now public video platforms long after the theatrical run was forgotten.
